# Open questions and limits

## Needs designer input before presenting this as real professional work

- Real name, positioning, availability, contact links and profile image.
- Six to nine real case studies, project dates, role, process and verifiable outcomes.
- Original media, appropriate captions/alt text and rights to publish it.
- Confirm the deliberate hybrid: readable platform feed beside decorative phone, rather than case studies clipped inside literal device screens.
- Confirm how strongly to emphasize nostalgic materials and whether platform-specific copy should diverge further between languages.

## Deliberate implementation choices

- CSS devices are stylized illustrations, not engineering-accurate replicas. The foldable uses two halves and a book hinge; no hardware-accuracy claim.
- Mobile has no simulated phone hardware. Recognition comes from the platform interface and chronological headings.
- The device preview is representative of the era; it does not mirror every currently visible project. All essential media is in the semantic feed.
- No actual AI, Twitter, Telegram, Tumblr or VK services, accounts, integrations or implied affiliation.
- Placeholder visuals are labeled abstract product illustrations, not real project screenshots or claimed achievements.
- Long copy increases native scroll length rather than hiding behind interactions.
- Language preservation is project-relative, not sentence-exact. Language switching within an era bridge preserves the relative era position.
- Video playback and external prototypes are optional. Include all essential narrative information in visible text and posters.

## Remaining validation limits

Playwright Chromium and WebKit are available. Physical iPhone Safari, dynamic address-bar collapse, VoiceOver listening, Android performance and prolonged low-power-device profiling require real hardware. Responsive viewport-resize tests approximate but cannot certify those conditions. No claim of a formal accessibility certification or measured 60fps on every device.
